# Águila Inventario Pro - Phase 4B: Architecture Analysis

## Current Model: Multi-Lote V3
- **Data Structure**: `productos/{determinante}/{codigoBarras}/lotes/{loteId}`.
- **Lote ID**: Deterministic hash generated by `b64(bodega + "_" + fechaCaducidad)`.
- **Atomicity**: `inventory-core.js` employs `firebase.database.Reference.transaction` for all stock modifications (`modificarStock`, `modificarStockMultiLote`, `guardarProducto`).
- **Stock Total**: Calculated as a summation of all active lotes.

## Proposed Model: Temporary Reception
To implement "RECEPCIÓN TEMPORAL" without a second system:
1. **Reserved Location**: Use a system-reserved bodega name: `🚚 POR ACOMODAR`.
2. **Implementation**: Treat "Temporary Reception" as a standard lote within the existing product structure. 
   - `loteId` will be `b64("🚚 POR ACOMODAR" + "_" + fechaCaducidad)`.
3. **Distribution Logic**: During audit of a physical bodega (e.g., "Bodega 12"), if the counted quantity exceeds the system stock for that bodega, the system will attempt a "Balanced Move" from `🚚 POR ACOMODAR`.

### Technical Contract
- **Invariant**: `Sum(lotes[i].stock) == stockTotal`.
- **Atomic Operation**: A single transaction must update both the target bodega lote and the `🚚 POR ACOMODAR` lote to ensure no stock is "lost in flight".
- **Path**: `productos/{det}/{code}/lotes/`.

## Concurrency and Distribution
- **Acumulation**: Multiple scans of the same product during reception will simply increment the `stock` of the `🚚 POR ACOMODAR` lote via transaction.
- **Partial Assignment**: The system supports partial distribution (e.g., moving 10 of 100 received boxes) by updating the stocks of both lotes in one atomic step.

## Performance Analysis
- **Read-Modify-Write**: Since lotes are part of the product object, we already have the necessary context in memory during a transaction. No extra database lookups are needed for balance checks.
- **Selective Rendering**: Update only the relevant product card in the UI after a reception event.
